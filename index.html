The four pillars

Credit repair is regulated for a reason. Lumina Credit is built around the work itself — accurate disputes, honest timelines, and a readiness measure your clients and partners can both understand — presented so people actually want to keep going.

📈 LuminaScore™

A readiness score that answers the only question that matters — how close is this client to fundable? Built from the negative items, utilization, payment standing, and dispute progress in the client's own file, with the math shown in plain language.

📱 Lumina Pocket™

The client's pocket companion — their plan, their readiness, and their progress on their phone, in one glance. Because most clients will never open a desktop dashboard.

💬 Response Decoder™

Bureau letters are written in legal static. The Decoder translates any response into plain language: what the bureau actually did, what it means for the plan, and the next step.

✨ Your Next Best Move™

No 40-item to-do list. Every client sees the single highest-impact action, always current — send this dispute, pay this card down, decode that reply. Momentum without overwhelm.

⏱️ Disputes, tracked honestly

Every dispute carries its bureau, sent date, and response window. Federal law gives bureaus 30–45 days to investigate; the platform shows that clock instead of pretending answers come overnight.

🗂️ Document Vault

Reports, letters, and bureau responses in one place, with a record trail on everything sent. In a regulated business, the paper trail is the product.

How It Works for Partners

From a deal stalled at the credit step to a client back in the Ready band — you enroll the client, the platform supports the readiness work, and you are alerted when they reach Ready so you can reconnect.

1

You enroll a client

A deal stalls at the credit step — a mortgage application, an auto loan, a lease. Instead of turning the client away, you enroll them in Lumina Credit the same day.

2

Report imported, plan built

The client imports a credit report. The platform finds the negative items, drafts FCRA dispute letters, and builds a step-by-step readiness plan around their goal.

3

Disputes run — fully tracked

Every dispute carries its bureau, mailed date, and the FCRA response window, and every bureau reply is decoded into plain language for the client.

4

You watch the pipeline board

Each client's stage, LuminaScore™, and current move at a glance — clients moving from Enrolled to In Plan to Disputes Running, without you chasing updates.

5

You're alerted at Nearly Ready and Ready

When a client crosses into the Nearly Ready or Ready band, the alert lands on your board — so you can reconnect with the client. Any lending decision remains the lender's.

The partner privacy boundary, plainly: partners see client progress — readiness, pipeline stage, and the current next move. You do not see the client's full credit report, account-by-account details, or dispute letter text. Progress is shared; the report stays the client's.

Plans

Your plan and price are set when you enroll — your partner or the Lumina team confirms the exact terms with you before anything is charged. Billing never starts before services are performed, and you can cancel at any time.

Lumina Credit

Lumina Credit provides credit education and dispute-preparation tools. LuminaScore™ is a readiness estimate based on the information in your file — it is not a credit score, and no outcome or score increase is guaranteed. Lenders make their own decisions. You have the right to dispute inaccurate information with the credit bureaus directly, for free, at annualcreditreport.com.

Before we start — the honest disclosures

  • LuminaScore™ is a readiness estimate, not a credit score. No score increase or funding outcome is guaranteed.
  • You can dispute inaccurate items with the bureaus yourself, free, at annualcreditreport.com. This platform organizes and tracks that work for you.
  • Bureaus generally have 30 days (up to 45) to investigate a dispute under the FCRA. Timelines shown here reflect that.
  • Under the Credit Repair Organizations Act, you may cancel within three business days of enrolling, and fees are never charged before services are performed.
